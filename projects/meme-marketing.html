India Restaurant Kolkata

Stepping into the Meme World Series

Social Virality

The Challenge

Standard social media food posts are ignored because audiences are tired of direct advertising, resulting in low organic reach.

Inside My Brain (The Strategy)

We spoke in our audience's language by aligning local culinary debates (such as wanting potato/aloo in Kolkata biryani) with famous film/TV memes, building massive community affinity.

The Copy Tactics & Hooks

Memes used: - Pizza vs Biryani (Gully Boy): Alia Bhatt holding a stick behind Vijay Varma: "Me when my friend says he'll rather have a pizza than Biryani." - Reality TV Despair (Shehnaaz Gill): "When I open Zomato/Swiggy and India Restaurant is unavailable for delivery (Kya karu main mar jau)." - Biryani Potato Rivalry (Richa Chadha): "When people don't want Aloo in their Biryani (Tum logo ko khana kaise hazam ho raha be?)." - Distracted Boyfriend: Staring at India Restaurant Biryani. - Brahmastra/ADHM Ranbir: "Person having India Restaurant's Biryani for the first time."
