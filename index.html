Reframing Clinical Anxiety as a Fitness Challenge

350+ Participants

Reframed a standard heart-testing screening from an anxiety-inducing check-up into an active, wellness-driven treadmill challenge.

The Challenge

Heart screenings suffer from low voluntary attendance because hospital environments, doctors, and tests trigger clinical anxiety.

Inside My Brain (The Strategy)

I completely removed standard hospital triggers. No pictures of wards, needles, or doctors. We reframed it around active fitness, using treadmill visual motifs and challenge-led copy to make it feel routine, approachable, and proactive.

The Copy Tactics

Headline: "Test Your Limits, Not Your Heart." I emphasized free registration, clear event timelines, and simple next steps to lower psychological and administrative entry barriers.

350+ Participants (5 days)

Regional Campaign

Reframed clinical infertility treatment from a cold, intimidating testing procedure into a warm, supportive path to motherhood.
